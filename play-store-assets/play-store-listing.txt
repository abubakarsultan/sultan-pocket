FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
-----------------------------------
Sultan Pocket is your all-in-one personal finance companion — built to help
you understand where your money goes and take control of it, without the
clutter of a traditional banking app.

TRACK EVERY RUPEE
Log income and expenses in seconds. See your balance update instantly, sort
by category, and export your transaction history to CSV whenever you need it.

BUDGET PLANNER
Set spending limits for each category and see exactly how you're tracking
against them in real time — before you overspend, not after.

SAVINGS GOALS
Set a target amount and a deadline for anything you're saving toward, and
watch your progress grow with every contribution.

BILL & SUBSCRIPTION REMINDERS
Never miss a due date again. Keep every recurring bill and subscription in
one place with clear reminders.

BUILT-IN AI ASSISTANT
Ask Sultan Pocket's assistant to add a transaction, check your balance, or
summarize your spending — in plain language.

PRIVATE & SECURE
Your data is tied to your account and protected with row-level security —
nobody else can see your numbers.

WORKS OFFLINE
Sultan Pocket is a installable app that keeps working even with a patchy
connection, and syncs the moment you're back online.

Whether you're managing a monthly salary, a side hustle, or your household
budget, Sultan Pocket gives you a clear, honest picture of your money —
your way.
